01 / Results at a glance

What the experiment shows.

Three pretrained runs share the same data and update count. Learning rates and trainable capacities differ, so these results do not isolate an architectural effect.

Reading the evidence. Lower CE means a better fit to target tokens. It does not establish improved academic tone or preserved meaning. All three checkpoints now have test predictions. Document groups before sentence splits.

The 80/10/10 policy applies to connected document groups. Sentence counts therefore differ from those proportions.

03 / Optimization

Read the trend through the noise.

Each update sees two training pairs. Raw mini-batch losses fluctuate; a trailing 20-step mean makes the local trend visible without hiding the observations.

Training time and peak allocated CUDA memory are observations from individual processes. They are not controlled efficiency benchmarks; peak allocation is not total VRAM usage.

04 / Validation study

A small, controlled learning-rate comparison.

Full-hybrid trials share initialization, data, seed, mini-batches and 300 updates. Final validation CE selects the learning rate within this limited comparison.

Checkpoint boundary. The runner saves final-step weights. A best observed validation loss does not imply that a best checkpoint was saved. Multi-seed comparisons and validation-based checkpoint selection remain pending.

05 / Meaning · academic style · fluency

Similarity is one dimension, not success.

Independent pretrained semantic models score the three checkpoints, a copy-source control and the human reference. These scores describe preservation; they cannot establish academic improvement.

Each numeric cell reports the mean and a 95% percentile interval from 1,000 paired resamples of 10 connected document groups. BERTScore is raw/unrescaled DistilRoBERTa layer 5, not a semantic-equivalence probability. NLI uses an independently pretrained DeBERTa classifier; the minimum of the two entailment probabilities is a generic diagnostic, not a reproduction of the published MIS model. Word edit rate has no universally optimal value.

Academic style: independent human Academic/Non-academic labels are needed before training/calibrating the classifier. Fluency and successful rewriting: independent ratings are pending. Missing dimensions remain unavailable. Human references are controls, not adjudicated guarantees of equivalent meaning. All these examples were already inspected.

07 / Provenance and next steps

Evidence you can trace.

This report reads saved logs and predictions, including the additional full-hybrid evaluation and the post-inspection output-length diagnostic. Checkpoints are unchanged.

Implemented and recorded

Real revision data; document-group splits; pretrained BART and hybrid training; saved checkpoints; deterministic generation; an inspected test comparison.

Still unverified

Academic-tone improvement, meaning preservation, official Mamba kernel parity, optimized speedups, and an architectural advantage under matched controls.

Next experiment

Independent tone and meaning labels; validation-based checkpoint selection; matched optimization and parameter controls; fusion ablations; multiple seeds; fresh held-out examples.
